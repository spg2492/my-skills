# PathFactory Context

PathFactory is a B2B content intelligence and activation platform that helps marketing and sales teams improve content strategies, engage prospects, and accelerate the buyer's journey by delivering relevant, personalized content experiences.

## Core Platform Capabilities

**Content Experience** — Interactive and personalized content experiences including recommendations, dynamic landing pages, and personalized content journeys.

**Content Insights** — Analytics on content consumption, engagement levels, and how content influences the buyer's journey.

**Buyer Enablement** — Easy access to the right content at every stage of the buying process, tailored to individual prospects and accounts.

**Account-Based Marketing (ABM)** — Personalized content experiences for specific target accounts and decision-makers.

**Integrations** — Connects with Salesforce, Marketo, HubSpot, and other marketing automation and CRM tools.

**Data and Analytics** — Detailed performance analytics for data-driven content decisions.

**Lead Acceleration** — Accelerates lead nurturing by serving relevant content based on prospect behavior.

**Content Recommendations** — ML and AI algorithms recommend the most relevant content to each individual user.

## Products

**Campaign Tools** — Accelerate the buyer journey by connecting buyers with the most-relevant content.

**Revenue Intelligence (PFRI)** — Deliver personalized content experiences, boost intent, and turn prospects into buyers fast.

**VEX (Virtual Events Hub)** — Nurture attendees with relevant content during digital events.

**Website Tools** — Add personalized sections to your website without sacrificing page layouts or requiring redesigns.

**ChatFactory** — A B2B buying agent powered by genAI that turns hard-to-navigate websites into self-serve buying experiences.

**Templated Experiences** — Standardized, on-brand content experience templates that scale personalization across teams.

**Content Playlists** — Curated sequences of content that guide buyers through a structured, bingeable journey.
